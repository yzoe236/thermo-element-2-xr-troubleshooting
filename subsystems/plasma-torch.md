## Plasma repeatedly shuts down automatically and related cooling system issues

*2026-02-03 · original list thread: “Plasma turning off repeatedly” · status: partial*

- **Symptom:** Element 2 plasma shuts down automatically after 60 minutes with no error message, then shuts down repeatedly every 30–60 minutes; Element XR plasma cannot ignite, load coil sensor indicator off, RF generator overheats causing exhaust blockage
- **Likely cause:** Coolant flow sensor failure; load coil water circulation blockage; insufficient water flow or sensor contamination; RF generator exhaust blockage; RF generator fan failure
- **Fix / suggestions:** Check and replace coolant flow sensor; clean or replace paddle wheel flow meters; unblock load coil water circulation; maximize water flow for cooling; check RF generator fan operation; clean exhaust passage; connector cables may need replacement

## Repeated plasma ignition failure—usual suspects cleared

*2024-05-24 · original list thread: “Plasma ignition problems - usual suspects seem innocent” · status: partial*

- **Symptom:** Element XR plasma unable to ignite. After replacing all torch gas lines, injector o-rings, bonnet, guard electrode, injector gas supply nipple o-rings, and 4mm union—still unsuccessful. New torch block and injector combo resolves issue. Simultaneously, Li7 tune desorption strength abnormally high (3× expected), suggesting excess N14++. Second day: new torch block unable to ignite again.
- **Likely cause:** Unconfirmed possibilities discussed on-list: an argon supply problem, chiller pressure (49 psi was reported versus a suggested 50-80 psi), load-coil alignment, excessive cooling-water conductivity, or coil deterioration. None was established as the root cause
- **Fix / suggestions:** Test different liquid Ar cylinders; replace cooling water; check load coil axial and transverse position; adjust chiller temperature; attempt ignition without intro system; diagnosis pending.

## Plasma ignition failure after torch replacement

*2024-05-02 · original list thread: “Plasma not igniting” · status: solved*

- **Symptom:** Unable to ignite plasma after torch replacement. Weak orange glow and spark sounds present, but RF power rises to 1250W. Torch assembly becomes very hot.
- **Likely cause:** Ignition cable connector corrosion; gas connection leak; torch ignition circuit design issue
- **Fix / suggestions:** Replace connector; readjust torch cable position and replace o-rings and injector gas connectors; replace connector to resolve. Subsequent issues (auto-extinguish after 25-30 min) resolved by checking cooling water temperature/flow, interface pump, turbopump oil pad maintenance.
