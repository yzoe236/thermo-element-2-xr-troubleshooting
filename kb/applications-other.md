## Feasibility of chloride determination in seawater by ICP

*2026-05-21 · original list thread: “Clorine with ICP” · status: partial*

- **Symptom:** Inquiry into whether ICP-OES or ICP-MS can replace ion chromatography for seawater chloride determination
- **Likely cause:** Not conclusively identified
- **Fix / suggestions:** ICP-OES requires a special 135 nm window and is prone to sensitivity loss; ICP-MS is feasible but requires high resolution; silver nitrate potentiometric titration is recommended as a more robust alternative approach
- **Credit:** asked by Siv Hjorth Dundas; answered by Louis A. Derry, Fernando Jiménez Barredo, Victoria Anderson, Steven R. Baum

## Feasibility of noble gas (Kr-85, Xe) measurement in Element 2

*2026-02-17 · original list thread: “noble gas analysis” · status: solved*

- **Symptom:** Need to analyze radioactive Kr and Xe isotopes on Element 2
- **Likely cause:** Not conclusively identified
- **Fix / suggestions:** Feasible. Multiple researchers have successfully analyzed Xe (including fission Xe in nuclear fuel rods); recommend dissolving Kr in ethanol mixed with 2% HNO3 for operation; or introduce as gas for higher ionization rate; Xe can be used as additional gas in ICP to eliminate polyatomic interference
- **Credit:** asked by Mark A. Tyra; answered by Richard, Paul Field, José Ignacio García Alonso, Robert S. Houk, Jeff Kahr, Rob Franks

## Jet interface performance analysis—sensitivity and vacuum pressure optimization

*2024-12-10 · original list thread: “Jet interface upgrade, machine sensitivity and HV pressure” · status: solved*

- **Symptom:** No problem; performance analysis inquiry. Jet cones show higher HV (~3.5e−7) vs standard cones (~2.5e−7). Sensitivity 5× below Thermo spec. Dual-interface pump boosts sensitivity 50%.
- **Likely cause:** Standard interface pump capacity insufficient for full Jet interface potential; suboptimal tune parameters
- **Fix / suggestions:** Upgrade to high-capacity Jet interface pump. Optimize Apex gas flow (N2 >5 mL/min, Ar >4 L/min), RF power, torch Z position. Additional pump (Edwards E2M30) partially improves sensitivity. Detailed parameter testing and analysis provided.
- **Credit:** asked by Jakub Sliwinski; answered by Jana Durisova
