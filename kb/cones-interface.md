## Mid-resolution Fe-ArO interference difficult separation ⏳

*2025-06-25 · original list thread: “Poor Fe-ArO peak separation in MR” · status: unresolved*

- **Symptom:** After slit replacement, Fe-56 ArO interference cannot separate; sensitivity and UO oxide percentage high; lens and parameter optimization limited; lower RF power improves but drastically reduces sensitivity
- **Likely cause:** New slit possible defect, optical element misalignment, or high Ar oxidation related
- **Fix / suggestions:** Check Matsuda plate voltage; adjust slit pressure and nebulizer gas; consider replacing slit; adjust Z position; UNRESOLVED in thread
- **Credit:** asked by Laramie Jensen; answered by Brian Dreyer, Maya REIMI, Siv Dundas

## Low-resolution sensitivity drop with mid-resolution unaffected ⏳

*2025-06-03 · original list thread: “sensitivity drop in LR but not MR” · status: unresolved*

- **Symptom:** After slit replacement, LR In sensitivity drops from 1.4e6 cps to 700K; week later drops to 350K; MR In and resolution unchanged
- **Likely cause:** Not conclusively identified; possible LR entrance/exit slit pressure improper
- **Fix / suggestions:** Adjust LR entrance and exit slit pressure; UNRESOLVED in thread
- **Credit:** asked by Thomas Marchitto; answered by Tim Wahl, Torsten Lindemann

## Element XR high-resolution performance degradation

*2025-03-19 · original list thread: “High resolution issues” · status: partial*

- **Symptom:** After slit component replacement, HR cannot reach 10000 m/z; plateau only 7000-8000; resolution decreases during scan; tailing at 39K increases
- **Likely cause:** Not conclusively identified; possible slit defect, optical contamination or charge accumulation, flight tube entrance aperture damage
- **Fix / suggestions:** Check flight tube entrance aperture (may require $3700 replacement); check transfer lens cleanliness; cross-compare slit components between two instruments for diagnosis
- **Credit:** asked by Martin Fleisher; answered by Lisa Stockli, Christopher Coath
